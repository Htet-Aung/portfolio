Htet also supplied `docs/my-uploads/NumPyGrad_Rover.mp4` and requested showing the rover movement. The public derivative is `public/media/numpygrad/rover-mission.mp4`: the full 5.833-second clip resized from 2558×1598 to 1600×1000, encoded as H.264/yuv420p with fast-start metadata. Its audio track was confirmed silent (maximum approximately −91 dB) and removed. The original upload remains unchanged. The derivative is 229,308 bytes, compared with 12,661,112 bytes for the original. `rover-poster.png` is the extracted first frame; Astro optimizes the displayed poster. The recording begins during the first turn and continues to the goal, rather than showing the entire mission from step zero. Authored route-description captions live in `rover-route.vtt`; the adjacent text describes the movement. Native controls and `preload="none"` keep playback user-initiated.
